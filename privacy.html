Privacy Policy

Effective date: 30 July 2026

This policy explains how information may be handled when you visit nextsolution.cc. Next Solution is an informational technology website hosted through GitHub Pages.

Information you provide

If you contact Next Solution through an external platform such as X, YouTube or GitHub, that platform processes your account information and message under its own privacy policy. Do not send passwords, authentication tokens, payment-card information or private device logs.

Server and technical information

Hosting providers and content-delivery services may process standard technical information needed to deliver and protect the site. This can include IP address, browser type, device type, requested page, referring page, time of request and diagnostic information.

Google AdSense and advertising cookies

Next Solution uses Google AdSense to display advertising. Google and its advertising partners may use cookies, web storage, device identifiers or similar technologies to serve, measure and personalize ads where permitted. These technologies may use information about visits to this and other websites.

Google’s use of advertising data is described in its advertising technologies information. Visitors may manage Google advertising personalization through My Ad Center.

Consent choices

Where required by law, a consent message may be displayed before certain advertising cookies or personalized ads are used. Your available options may include accepting, rejecting or managing purposes and vendors. Non-personalized ads can still use limited information such as the current page, approximate location and device type.

External links and embedded content

The site links to services such as YouTube, X and GitHub. If you follow an external link or interact with embedded content, the third party may receive technical information and apply its own privacy practices. Next Solution does not control those services.

Data retention

Next Solution does not operate a user-account database on this website. Messages sent through external platforms are retained according to the platform’s settings and may be kept as long as necessary to answer a request, maintain support history or address abuse.

Children’s privacy

The site is intended for a general audience and is not designed to collect personal information from children. A child should not submit personal information through external contact channels.

Your choices

  • Use browser controls to block or delete cookies.
  • Adjust advertising personalization through Google’s controls.
  • Decline optional consent choices when a consent message is shown.
  • Avoid submitting personal information through external contact services.

Policy updates

This policy may be updated when site features, advertising services or legal requirements change. The effective date at the top identifies the latest published version.

Contact

For a privacy question, use the methods listed on the contact page.
