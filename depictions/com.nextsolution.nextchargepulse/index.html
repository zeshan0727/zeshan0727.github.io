Next Charge Pulse

One clear success haptic whenever your iPhone transitions from battery power to external power.

iOS 15+RootlessRootHideNo settings

How it works

  • Responds only to a real charger connection.
  • Does not pulse repeatedly while charging or when reaching 100%.
  • Does not create a false haptic after respring.

Privacy and safety

The tweak injects only into SpringBoard, stores no data and does not access messages, clipboard contents, credentials, files, contacts, location or network traffic.
