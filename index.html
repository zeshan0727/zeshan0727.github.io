Our approach

  • Use customization tools only on devices you own or are authorized to manage.
  • Back up important data before system-level changes.
  • Avoid pirated packages, cracked applications and unknown repositories.
  • Check iOS version, architecture and jailbreak type before installation.
  • Prefer reversible changes and test one modification at a time.
